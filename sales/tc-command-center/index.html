Common questions

Do I need to understand Google Apps Script?

No. The core toolkit works without scripts. Start with the workbook. Scripts are optional source files for buyers who specifically want to configure reminders in Google Sheets.

Does it automatically know my contract deadlines?

No. You enter transaction-specific dates from the signed contract or responsible professional. The toolkit is an organizational system and does not determine legal or contractual deadlines.

Is this legal, tax, lending, or financial advice?

No. It is an organizational workflow product. Transaction-specific professional questions should be handled by the appropriate broker, attorney, lender, title/escrow professional, tax adviser, or other qualified professional.
